# Zer0Life Intellectual Growth
## 12-month reading and practice pathway

A multilingual learning pathway for the Zer0Life Book Club. The plan is designed around one core book or substantial primary text per month, weekly notes, and one practical exercise. Book titles are recommendations, not a representation that a particular translation or edition is cleared for commercial redistribution.

## Learning outcomes
By the end of the year, a reader should be better able to:
- explain ideas clearly in speech and writing;
- identify weak arguments, cognitive biases, and unsupported claims;
- read critically across philosophy, psychology, business, and society;
- communicate, negotiate, and lead more deliberately;
- build a personal learning system and apply ideas in real projects.

## Monthly pathway

| Month | Theme | Core reading suggestions | Practice / milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learning how to think | René Descartes, *Discourse on the Method*; John Stuart Mill, *On Liberty* | Keep a question journal; separate facts, interpretations, and assumptions. |
| 2 | Logic and critical thinking | Aristotle, *Organon* (selected works); open textbook *Critical Thinking* (CC BY edition to be individually verified) | Reconstruct one argument per day; identify premises, conclusion, and evidence. |
| 3 | Rhetoric and eloquence | Aristotle, *Rhetoric*; Cicero, *De Oratore*; Quintilian, *Institutio Oratoria* (selected books) | Prepare a 3-minute speech; practise ethos, logos, pathos, structure, and audience awareness. |
| 4 | Writing and communication | William Strunk Jr., *The Elements of Style*; George Orwell, *Politics and the English Language* | Write a one-page explanation, edit for clarity, then present it aloud. |
| 5 | Psychology and decision-making | William James, *The Principles of Psychology* (selected chapters); Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* | Maintain a decision diary; note uncertainty and possible biases. |
| 6 | Emotions, relationships, and wellbeing | Epictetus, *Enchiridion*; modern evidence-based psychology readings selected by the editorial team | Practise active listening; distinguish psychological evidence from unsupported psychosomatic claims. |
| 7 | Personal effectiveness | Benjamin Franklin, *Autobiography*; Charles Duhigg, *The Power of Habit* | Build one sustainable habit with a weekly review. |
| 8 | Business and entrepreneurship | Adam Smith, *The Wealth of Nations* (selected chapters); Peter Drucker, *The Practice of Management* | Draft a one-page business model and customer problem statement. |
| 9 | Marketing and persuasion | Claude Hopkins, *Scientific Advertising*; modern marketing case studies selected by editors | Create a customer profile, value proposition, and ethical campaign brief. |
| 10 | Management and leadership | Frederick Winslow Taylor, *The Principles of Scientific Management*; Peter Drucker, *The Effective Executive* | Run a small project with goals, roles, feedback, and retrospective. |
| 11 | Money, economics, and strategy | Adam Smith, *The Wealth of Nations*; Benjamin Graham, *The Intelligent Investor* | Write an investment decision checklist; learn risk, diversification, and opportunity cost. |
| 12 | Synthesis and public speaking | Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations*; selected speeches by Abraham Lincoln and other historical speakers | Deliver a 5-minute talk and publish a personal 12-month learning review. |

## Weekly rhythm
1. Read for 4 sessions of 25–40 minutes.
2. Write a 150–250 word summary in your own words.
3. Capture 3 useful ideas and 1 question that remains unresolved.
4. Complete one practical exercise.
5. At the end of the month, give a short spoken explanation without reading notes.

## Editorial and rights policy
- Keep modern copyrighted titles as recommendations or external library/retailer links unless written permission or a suitable commercial redistribution licence is documented.
- A public-domain author does not automatically make every modern translation, edition, annotation, cover, or digitisation public domain. Check the exact edition and the relevant territories before importing full text.
- Creative Commons material must be checked at the work and version level. CC BY and CC BY-SA may permit commercial reuse subject to conditions; CC BY-NC does not permit commercial use; CC BY-ND restricts adaptations.
- Store source URL, exact edition, translator/editor, licence, territory, evidence URL, and review date for every imported work.
- Keep uncertain items in rights review; never publish them in the paid reader until cleared.

## Language strategy
The pathway is available as a curriculum in Ukrainian, Russian, Polish, and English. Prefer original-language works where possible. For translations, record the translator and independently verify the translation's rights; do not assume a translation is free because the original work is public domain.

## Product experience
Each monthly module should have:
- a premium original Zer0Life cover for the module;
- a concise editorial introduction and learning objectives;
- book cards with author, language, edition, category, and rights status;
- reading progress, notes, bookmarks, and a monthly completion badge;
- a short original Zer0Life exercise or discussion prompt.

This curriculum is an educational pathway, not medical advice or a promise to increase measured intelligence. Psychosomatic topics should be handled carefully and should not replace professional medical assessment.
