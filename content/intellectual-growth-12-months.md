# Zer0Life Intellectual Growth — 12-month reading path

A practical year-long learning path for Zer0Life Book Club members. The sequence moves from clear thinking and communication to psychology, business, leadership and applied strategy.

## Learning principles
- Read 20–30 minutes a day, five days a week.
- Keep a one-page note for each chapter: main idea, evidence, objection, practical use.
- Every month, produce one visible output: a speech, decision memo, marketing experiment, team plan or personal reflection.
- Separate evidence-based psychology and health information from historical theories and self-help claims.
- A book title in this plan is a recommendation, not proof that a particular translation or digital edition may be redistributed commercially.

## The 12-month sequence

| Month | Theme | Core reading direction | Practical output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical thinking | Logic, fallacies, evidence, cognitive biases; Aristotle's *Rhetoric* as a historical text | Analyse one persuasive claim and identify its evidence and assumptions |
| 2 | Rhetoric and eloquence | Aristotle, Cicero's *De Oratore*, argument structure and speech craft | Deliver a 3-minute speech with a clear thesis and supporting arguments |
| 3 | Writing and communication | Clear prose, storytelling, listening, difficult conversations | Write a one-page persuasive memo and revise it twice |
| 4 | Psychology | Attention, memory, learning, emotion, decision-making; William James as a historical foundation | Keep a decision journal and review recurring thinking errors |
| 5 | Self-development and habits | Goal-setting, discipline, motivation, reflection | Build a 30-day habit experiment with a measurable baseline |
| 6 | Psychosomatics and wellbeing | Stress physiology, sleep, pain science, mind-body interaction; distinguish evidence from unsupported claims | Create a personal stress map and a list of questions for qualified clinicians when needed |
| 7 | Love and relationships | Attachment research, empathy, boundaries, conflict repair, communication | Practise one structured conversation and reflect on listening quality |
| 8 | Business foundations | Value creation, customers, business models, economics; Adam Smith as historical context | Draft a one-page business model and interview five potential customers |
| 9 | Marketing and sales | Positioning, customer research, copywriting, ethical persuasion, measurement | Run a small, measurable marketing test and report the result |
| 10 | Management and operations | Systems, priorities, delegation, process design; Frederick Taylor as historical context | Map one process and propose a measurable improvement |
| 11 | Leadership | Decision-making, trust, feedback, accountability, team culture | Write a leadership principles page and a 30-day team action plan |
| 12 | Strategy and integration | Long-term thinking, economics, risk, ethics, synthesis | Present a 10-minute strategy briefing supported by evidence |

## Suggested reading shelf

### Reasoning, rhetoric and communication
- Aristotle — *Rhetoric*
- Cicero — *De Oratore*
- Arthur Schopenhauer — *The Art of Being Right* (read critically as a study of disputation, not as a guide to honest dialogue)
- William Strunk Jr. — *The Elements of Style* (check edition and rights before any full-text distribution)

### Psychology and self-development
- William James — *The Principles of Psychology*
- Foundational and current evidence-based material on cognition, learning, habits, stress and relationships should be selected after checking the exact edition, author credentials and evidence quality.

### Business, marketing and management
- Adam Smith — *The Wealth of Nations*
- Frederick Winslow Taylor — *The Principles of Scientific Management*
- Claude C. Hopkins — *Scientific Advertising*
- Henry George — *Progress and Poverty*

### Love and relationships
- Curate evidence-based material on attachment, communication, boundaries and conflict resolution. Do not present one popular relationship framework as universal scientific fact.

## Editorial and rights rules
1. Only publish a full text when the exact text, edition, translation, cover and commercial redistribution rights have been verified for the markets served.
2. Public-domain status of an original work does not automatically make a modern translation, introduction, annotations, typesetting or cover public domain.
3. Keep uncertain items as metadata-only candidates or link to the source; never expose them as downloadable paid content.
4. Create original Zer0Life summaries, exercises and learning notes from scratch. Do not reproduce protected translations or long passages.
5. For psychosomatics, clearly distinguish established stress-related mechanisms from claims that specific emotions cause specific diseases. Encourage professional medical assessment for symptoms.

## Member progress record
For each month, track:
- Reading days completed
- Key concepts understood
- One argument the reader can explain in their own words
- One practical experiment
- One reflection on what changed

**Completion standard:** the member can explain the main ideas, identify limitations, and apply at least one idea responsibly—not merely finish the book.
